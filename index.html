Who we are

Built by players,
for players

Gateway Smash started as an answer to the corporate leagues in St. Louis: high fees, inconsistent play, and disorganized management. It is volunteer-run, and every dollar collected goes to court rental. Matches are indoors at Arch Pickleball, so a forecast never cancels a night. Running on free tools is what keeps the fee down, and all skill levels play.

Inclusive and player first

Players range from 2.0 to 4.0+. If you are still working out where to stand there is a pod for you, and if you are chasing a 4.0 there is a pod for that too.

How the ladder works

You play three doubles matches a night in a pod of four, partnering with each of the other three once. Elo ratings update weekly, so pods stay close to your level, and beating someone rated above you moves you the most.

Off the court

The WhatsApp group is where subs get found and where the after-match conversation happens. Scores live in Global Pickleball Network.
